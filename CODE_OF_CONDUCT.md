# Code of Conduct

We want collaboration here to be precise, respectful, and safe for people who
report mistakes in code, legal annotations, or evaluation claims.

Participants must:

- discuss evidence and behavior without personal attacks or harassment;
- respect reviewer confidentiality and never attempt to identify blinded reviewers;
- avoid publishing personal data, credentials, private transcripts, or embargoed reports;
- state uncertainty and disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- correct misleading benchmark, certification, or legal-validity claims promptly.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates these rules and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report sensitive conduct
issues through GitHub private vulnerability reporting rather than a public issue.
Good-faith criticism of the project, its data, or its conclusions is welcome.
